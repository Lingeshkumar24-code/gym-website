GymVerse AI – Smart Fitness & Workout Assistant
🏋️ Project Overview

GymVerse AI is a modern, responsive, and interactive fitness website designed to help users achieve their health and fitness goals through personalized workout plans, diet recommendations, and AI-powered guidance.

Inspired by modern fitness platforms and user-centered design principles, GymVerse AI combines a professional gym website with an intelligent AI fitness assistant that acts as a virtual personal trainer.

The platform provides a seamless experience for fitness enthusiasts, beginners, and athletes by offering exercise guidance, nutritional advice, membership registration, and personalized fitness recommendations.
